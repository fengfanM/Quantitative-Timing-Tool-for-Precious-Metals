# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, professional, and constructive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Give feedback with evidence and context.
- Discuss financial risk clearly and responsibly.
- Assume good intent, but correct misleading claims quickly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Promoting guaranteed investment returns.
- Deliberately hiding strategy risks, data limitations, or backtest caveats.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from participating in the project.
